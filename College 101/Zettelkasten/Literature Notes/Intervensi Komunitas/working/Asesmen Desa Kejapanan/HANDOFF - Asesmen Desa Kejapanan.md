## Tabeyan TPS conflict

The interview account is:

1. Tabeyan initially lacked a TPS.
2. Residents pooled money to provide one.
3. A pesantren later acquired land near or in front of the TPS.
4. The TPS was demolished in connection with preparations for a pengajian involving Gus Idham.
5. The TPS was not immediately rebuilt for almost three months.
6. Residents protested.
7. Residents accepted demolition only if the pesantren built a replacement.
8. The pesantren ultimately rebuilt the TPS, with santri participating in construction.

Analytical importance:

- the pesantren has meaningful local power;
- residents can mobilize collectively around a concrete shared resource;
- local power remains contestable;
- the conflict is resolved and should not be presented as ongoing.
