1. **High impact, anonymity risk. Location: §2.1, interview row, line 32.**  
   Listing “wakil ketua, sekretaris, dan bendahara” identifies three specific officeholders in a named village organization. The interview date and venue further narrow identification. The interview note explicitly records an anonymity promise and a public vault. Removing names therefore does not adequately anonymize the respondents, particularly when their comments concern identifiable colleagues. **Correction direction:** describe respondents collectively without enumerating unique offices, and reduce unnecessary identifying interview details.

2. **High impact, unresolved conflict with field evidence. Location: identity table, §1.2, and Tabel 3.1C, lines 7, 24, and 61.**  
   The repeated claim “belum pernah menjalankan program sendiri sejak dibentuk” is stronger than the interview evidence permits. The interview note, “Kegiatan selama ini,” records a child-designed competition at Festival Harmoni, adult reductions to its scope, distributed invitations, and attendance from several dusun. The assignment itself acknowledges this program in Tabel 3.1B. This affects the central problem being assessed. **Correction direction:** distinguish absence of an independently organized program from an existing child-initiated activity whose decisions were constrained by adults. Explain how the newer evidence qualifies the earlier fishbone.

3. **High impact, competency and causality claims exceed the method. Location: §1.1, §2.1, §3.3, and §3.4, lines 18, 36, 78–80, and 90.**  
   “Belum pernah mengadakan rapat” and “belum pernah merekrut anggota” establish lack of reported practice, not inability. Using an intermediary does not establish deficient speaking confidence. Nevertheless, the synthesis concludes “belum mampu” and asserts that these deficiencies “membuat forum tidak dapat bergerak bahkan ketika orang dewasa memberi ruang.” The unsupported prediction that inactive members have larger gaps extends this inference to people not interviewed. **Correction direction:** distinguish reported knowledge gaps, untested skills, organizational barriers, and proposed learning needs. Remove the causal certainty and prediction about inactive members unless supported by additional evidence.

4. **Material regulatory distinction is blurred. Location: §1.1 and §3.2, lines 18 and 69, continuing into §3.3’s recruitment row.**  
   “Rekrutmen yang sukarela dan berseleksi” and “seleksi anggota serta pengurus berikutnya” conflate ordinary membership with selection for office. The regulation distinguishes voluntary membership under Pasal 17, transcript page 12, from selection of *pengurus* under Pasal 22, pages 13–14. This wording could turn officeholder selection procedures into an unnecessary membership barrier. **Correction direction:** separate voluntary member recruitment from selection of officeholders, including the relevant responsibilities and procedures.

5. **Material omission in the replacement recommendation. Location: §3.4, non-training row and explanatory paragraph, lines 91 and 93.**  
   The recommendation mentions inactivity and approval by at least half the officeholders, but omits Pasal 23(3): replacements must come from the same *Kelompok Anak*. Pasal 23(4) also specifies 50% of the **entire** *kepengurusan*, not merely active officers or meeting attendees. These provisions appear on transcript page 14. **Correction direction:** include the replacement-origin requirement and make the denominator explicit. The stated reasons for replacement and percentage are otherwise supported.

6. **Material continuity error. Location: §1.2, target-group column, line 24.**  
   “Dalam *actor map*, Forum Anak berada pada kelompok berpengaruh rendah dan berkepentingan tinggi” conflicts with the prior assessment’s §4.3, row 11, which assigns low power and low interest. Its following paragraph explicitly labels inactivity an imperfect proxy for interest. The new interview could justify revising that assessment, but the assignment presents the revision as an existing result. **Correction direction:** identify high interest as an updated interpretation based on the interview, and explain the change from the earlier provisional classification.

7. **Material breaks in the KSA chain. Location: §3.2–§3.4, lines 69–80 and 90.**  
   Several components do not trace consistently across the tables:
   - The recruitment attitude “terbuka terhadap anggota baru dari semua dusun” is absent from §3.2. Its baseline, “sudah menginginkan regenerasi,” assesses a different attitude, while its proposed intervention strengthens an existing asset.
   - Advocacy confidence appears as an ideal in §3.3, whereas §3.2 specifies initiative. Waiting for invitations does not establish low confidence.
   - Feedback practice and the regeneration follow-up plan in §3.3 have no explicit counterpart in §3.4’s training recommendation.
   - Ordinary members receive distinct standards in §3.2, but §3.3 applies officer-derived baselines to the combined participant group despite §2.1’s limitation.

   **Correction direction:** preserve the same competencies through standard, evidence, gap, and recommendation. Separate established strengths from deficits, and mark unassessed member competencies as unknown.

8. **Evidence boundary is crossed in specific factual claims. Location: Tabel 3.1A, facilities row, line 48, and §3.3, advocacy baseline, line 80.**  
   “Ruang rapat desa tidak dapat dipakai” has no supporting statement in the supplied interview record, decisions, or prior assessment. Likewise, “kepala desa sebagai satu-satunya pendukung” strengthens evidence that respondents viewed the village head as supportive and failed to recognize Pak Eki’s usefulness. Neither establishes exclusivity. **Correction direction:** remove these stronger formulations or connect them to explicit evidence already authorized for use.

9. **Participant estimate changes meaning. Location: §3.3, first target-participant cell, line 78.**  
   The assignment describes school-attending members as “sekitar sepertiga anggota dalam SK.” The interview note instead estimates that one-third “masih dapat diajak.” Availability and school attendance are different criteria. This changes the estimated training population. **Correction direction:** retain the estimate’s original meaning and keep the number of eligible school-attending participants unspecified unless established separately.

10. **Minor APA defects. Location: Tabel 3.1B, line 55, and Daftar Pustaka, lines 99–103.**  
    The quotation “Maju Desanya Sejahtera Warganya” matches the [village vision page](https://sites.google.com/view/kantor-desa-kejapanan/profil-desa/visi-dan-misi), but its citation lacks a locator for a direct quotation from an unpaginated source. The reference list also places *Peraturan…* before *Pemerintah…*, reversing their alphabetical order. **Correction direction:** supply an appropriate section or paragraph locator and reorder the entries.

11. **Low impact, editorial judgment with a certainty problem. Location: §3.4, closing sentence of the regulatory paragraph, line 93.**  
    “Pelatihan menyiapkan pengurus untuk menggunakan ruang tersebut, sedangkan kesepakatan pembinaan memastikan ruang itu benar-benar dibuka” uses a polished parallel closure without specifying what “ruang” means operationally. “Memastikan” also makes an agreement sound sufficient to guarantee implementation. The stylistic objection is subjective, but the guarantee exceeds the evidence. **Correction direction:** name the concrete access or support intended and describe the agreement as a proposed enabling condition.
