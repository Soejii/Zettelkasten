## The emergence of qualitative research in psychology
Qualitative approaches have been part of psychology since its inception
- early 20th century - behaviorism adopted an experimentalist, positivist paradigm
- second half of 20th century - cognitive psychology continued experimentalist, positivist paradigm
- 1980s - rise of postmodern, social constructionist and phenomenological critiques of positivism and rise of qualitative approaches (apa betul manusai itu hanya digerakan secara mekanistik saja? apakah tidak ada pengaruh lain? awal mula manusia dijadikan sebagai suatu subjek) mengkiritk aliran positivistik sebelumnya

##  Characteristic of QUalitative research

### The characteristics of qualitative research (Howitt, 2010)
- Concern with the richness of description
- capturing the individual perspective
- the rejection of positivism and the use of post-modern perspectives
- adherence to the postmodern sensibility

#### key difference
quantitative
- hypothesis testing
- deductive (topdown/theory driven) approach
- aims to establish significant differences or correlational relationships
- aims to establish reliable and generalisable patterns of thought behavior

qualitative
- exploratory research questions
- inductive (bottom up/data driven)
- aims to provide rich description and make sense of a topic
- aims to examine meaning of topic within natural changing context
