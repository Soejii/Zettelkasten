1. **Factual error: qualitative coding sequence.**  
   **Location:** “Analisis data,” qualitative column, line 21: “dilakukan dalam dua putaran, kemudian skema pengodean ditinjau oleh peneliti kedua.”  
   **Evidence:** Weber et al. (2019, p. 3) place AL’s review **before** JW’s second coding round.  
   **Why it matters:** The current sequence misrepresents how methodological checking informed the analysis.  
   **Correction direction:** Place the second researcher’s review between the initial coding and the second coding round.

2. **Missing instrument definitions and Indonesian explanations.**  
   **Location:** “Metode,” “Pendekatan yang digunakan,” and “Hasil penelitian,” lines 18–22.  
   **Evidence:** “skor PHQ-4” appears without an instrument name or explanation. The reported stress means are not identified as PSQ-20 scores. Heinen et al.    **Why it matters:** Readers cannot clearly connect the numerical findings to their measurements, and the methodological comparison assumes unexplained terminology.  
   **Correction direction:** Identify the instruments and what they measure in the methods cell; briefly explain the principal methodological terms in Indonesian at first use while retaining their English names.

4. **Imprecise placement of thematic saturation.**  
   **Location:** “Pendekatan yang digunakan,” qualitative column, line 19: “subkategori dikembangkan secara induktif dari data sampai mencapai kejenuhan.”  
   **Evidence:** Weber et al. (2019, p. 2) report conducting focus groups until data saturation. Their analysis description on page 3 separately explains inductive category development and two coding rounds.  
   **Why it matters:** The sentence conflates the stopping criterion for data collection with completion of category development.  
   **Correction direction:** Attach saturation to focus-group data collection and describe inductive subcategory development separately.
